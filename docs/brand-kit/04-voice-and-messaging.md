# Voice and messaging

## Voice in four words
Precise. Clear. Confident. Calm.
We sound like the best builder you have ever hired: knows the answer, explains it simply, never oversells.

## Tone by situation
| Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website headline | Confident, visual | "See your new kitchen before we build it." |
| Trade page intro | Expert, specific | "Hardwood, engineered wood and LVP, installed flat, tight and square." |
| Estimate or proposal | Exact, transparent | "Line 4: Remove and dispose of existing carpet, 640 sq ft." |
| Project update | Brief, reassuring | "Framing inspection passed today. Drywall starts Thursday." |
| Delay or problem | Direct, accountable | "The tile shipment is late. New install date is the 14th. Nothing else moves." |
| Review reply | Warm, short | "Thank you, Dana. It was a pleasure working on your home." |

## Vocabulary
**Use:** precise, clear, exact, detailed, measured, see it first, on schedule, every step, one team, finished, built right, line by line, walkthrough, estimate.

**Never use in customer-facing material:** AI, artificial intelligence, powered by, smart (except the product category "smart home"), algorithm, cutting-edge, innovative, next-gen, revolutionary, disrupt, future, synergy, world-class, #1, best in Atlanta, cheap, affordable (say "fair price" instead).

**Say this, not that**
| Say | Not |
| --- | --- |
| Concept Preview | AI rendering |
| Estimate within 48 hours | Fast quotes |
| One team for the whole project | Full-service solutions provider |
| Updates at every stage | Real-time project tracking platform |
| Fair, clear pricing | Cheap, affordable, low price |

## The quiet tech test
Before anything ships, ask: would this still make sense coming from a top craftsman who is simply extremely organized? If yes, it fits. If it sounds like a startup, rewrite it.

## Tagline
Primary: **See it first. Built right.**
Approved alternates: "Seen first. Built right." / "Precise from plan to finish." / "One team. Every trade. Built right."

## Elevator pitch (15 seconds)
Solvern Home is a metro Atlanta construction company that handles 23 trades with one team. Send us a photo of your space and we will show you what it can look like, with an estimate, before any work begins.

## Boilerplate (about us)
Solvern Home serves homeowners across metro Atlanta with one accountable team for 23 trades, from roofing and flooring to plumbing, landscaping and complete renovations. Every project starts with a Concept Preview, so homeowners see the result before work begins, and runs on clear estimates, measured timelines and updates at every stage.

## Headline formulas
- Trade page: "[Result the homeowner wants], built right." Example: "Floors that stay flat, tight and quiet, built right."
- Concept Preview: "See your new [thing] before we build it."
- Hub: "One team for every part of your home."
- Project page: "[Room or project] remodels, from first photo to final walkthrough."

| --- | --- |

Button text is sentence case, with no arrows or exclamation marks.

## Writing mechanics
- Sentence case for headings, buttons and navigation.
- Numerals for all numbers ("3 days," "48 hours").
- Dates: "Thursday, March 14." Times: "9 am."
- Phone: (404) 555-0123 format.
- No em dashes; use a colon, comma or period.
- Oxford comma.
- Contractions are fine ("we'll," "you're").
