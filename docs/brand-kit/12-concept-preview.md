# Concept Preview

The signature offer. A homeowner sends a photo and a short description; Solvern sends back a concept image of the finished project and an estimate. Done by the team, not automated, for now.

## Name and language
- Always "Concept Preview." Never "AI rendering," "render" or "mockup" in customer-facing material.
- Headline pattern: "See your new [thing] before we build it."
- Button: "Get my Concept Preview."

## Where it applies
Visual trades: fencing, landscaping, painting, siding, roofing, flooring, tile, countertops, windows and doors, garage doors, masonry, concrete driveways, carpentry (decks, porches, cabinets), specialty, and all hub project types.
Diagnosis trades route to a call instead: plumbing, electrical, HVAC, low voltage. Their pages say "Call for a diagnosis."

## The form
1. Photo upload (1 to 5 photos).
2. Trade or project (dropdown, preselected on trade pages).
3. "What would you like?" (short text).
4. Zip code.
5. Name, phone, email.
6. Preferred contact method.
Button: "Get my Concept Preview." Confirmation: "Received. Your Concept Preview and estimate will arrive within 48 hours."

## Turnaround promise
Concept image and estimate within 48 hours, Monday through Saturday. Only change this promise if the team can meet the new time every time.

## What the customer receives
- The concept image next to their original photo, labeled "Concept Preview."
- An estimate range with the main line items.
- The next step: "Book your free site visit to confirm measurements and materials."
- Caption on every image: "Concept image for planning. Final materials and measurements are confirmed at your site visit."

## Internal production standard
- Concept images are produced internally with image-generation tools, then reviewed by a team member for realism, correct materials and nothing impossible to build.
- Keep the customer's house recognizable: same angle, same structure, only the requested change.
- Estimates are ranges until the site visit.
- Log every request, sent date and outcome in the job tracker.
