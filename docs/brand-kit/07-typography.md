# Typography

## Typefaces
| Role | Family | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display and headings | **Archivo** (Google Fonts), weights 600 and 700, width 112 where supported | A grotesque with industrial roots and a slightly wide stance, like job-site signage and stenciled equipment |
| Body and interface | **Public Sans** (Google Fonts), weights 400 and 600 | Plain, sturdy and very readable at small sizes on phones |
Both are free for commercial use. Fallback stack: Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.

## Type scale
| Style | Font | Size / line height | Weight | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display-xl | Archivo | 72 / 72 | 700 | Hub hero only |
| display-l | Archivo | 56 / 60 | 700 | Trade page hero |
| heading-1 | Archivo | 40 / 44 | 700 | Page titles |

| heading-3 | Archivo | 20 / 26 | 600 | Card and group titles |
| dimension | Archivo | 12 / 16 | 500 | Measurements on dimension lines, tabular figures |
| body-l | Public Sans | 18 / 28 | 400 | Intros and lead paragraphs |
| body | Public Sans | 16 / 26 | 400 | Default text |
| body-s | Public Sans | 14 / 22 | 400 | Captions, helper text |
| label | Public Sans | 14 / 20 | 600 | Form labels, badges |
| button | Public Sans | 16 / 20 | 600 | Buttons |
On mobile, reduce display-xl to 44/48 and display-l to 38/42; everything else stays.

## Rules
- Sentence case for all headings and labels. Only the logo is set in capitals.
- Left aligned. Center only single-line headlines in the hero or on print pieces.
- Line length 45 to 75 characters for body text.
- Use tabular figures for prices, measurements and dates in estimates.
- Never more than two weights on one screen besides the logo.
- Never letter-space body text. Display sizes use the negative tracking in the tokens.
