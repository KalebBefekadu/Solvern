# Brand architecture and naming

## Model: branded house
One master brand, **Solvern**, carries every trade. Each trade keeps its own color and 3D tool, but the name, type, layout, voice and logo never change. Think of one company with specialists, not a collection of separate companies.

## Name structure
| Level | Format | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company (hub) | Solvern Home | Solvern Home |
| Trade | Solvern + trade name | Solvern Roofing, Solvern Tile |
| Project type (hub pages) | Solvern + project | Solvern kitchen remodels |
| Offer | Concept Preview | "Get your Concept Preview" |

## Trade name rules
- Use the short trade name in the lockup and headlines: Solvern Flooring, Solvern HVAC, Solvern Landscape.
- Use the full trade name in page titles and navigation: Landscaping and irrigation, Windows and doors.
- Never abbreviate Solvern (no "SLV," no "Solv").
- Never add another brand name or sub-brand.

## Short names for lockups
Demolition, Site Work, Concrete, Masonry, Carpentry, Roofing, Siding, Windows & Doors, Garage Doors, Insulation, Drywall, Painting, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Smart Home, Flooring, Tile, Countertops, Landscape, Tree Service, Fencing, Specialty.

## Pronunciation and spelling
Say it **SOL-vurn**. On the phone, spell it once: "Solvern, S-O-L-V-E-R-N." Always one word, capital S, never "SolVern" or "Sol Vern."

## Legal and business names
- The LLC operates Solvern Home as a registered trade name in Georgia.
- Legal names appear only on contracts, invoices and required legal text. Marketing always says Solvern or Solvern Home.

## Domains and handles
- Primary domain: solvern.com if available, otherwise solvernhome.com.
- Trade pages live on the one domain: solvern.com/flooring, solvern.com/roofing.
- Social handles: @solvernhome on every platform.
- Buy common misspellings when inexpensive (for example, solvurn.com) and redirect them to the main domain.

## Color families
The 23 trades are grouped into six families so customers learn where each trade lives in the house. See Trade directory for every trade's color and tool.
| Family | Base token | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Groundwork | `clay` | The ground, the foundation, what everything stands on |
| Structure & Shell | `slate` | The frame and the skin of the house |
| Interior Finishes | `gold` | The surfaces you see and touch every day |
| Home Systems | `verdigris` | What runs inside the walls: power, water, air, data |
| Outdoor Living | `leaf` | The yard and its edges |
| Specialty | `plum` | Custom and specialty installations |
The hub, Solvern Home, uses `ink` with the `chalk-blue` accent and shows all families together.
