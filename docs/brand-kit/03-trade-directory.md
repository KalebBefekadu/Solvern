## Scope writing rules
- Lead with what the homeowner gets, then list the work.
- Use the homeowner's words ("backsplash," not "wall tile substrate").
- Each scope item appears under one trade only. Waterproofing is split: foundation waterproofing under Concrete, shower waterproofing under Tile. Stone veneer lives under Masonry. Hauling lives under Demolition.
