# Logo

## The mark: the mitered S
A squared S drawn as two pieces joined by a single mitered cut through its center, the joint a carpenter makes when two pieces meet at an angle. It says two things at once: precise geometry and hands-on craft. The squared corners echo a framing square.

## Trade lockups
Wordmark on top, trade short name below in Archivo Bold, separated by a 4-unit bar in that trade's `trade-*` color. The bar is the only place trade color touches the logo. Build every trade from the flooring template.

## Clear space
Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the mark's top bar (one sixth of the mark's height) on every side, and at least the full mark width between the logo and any other logo.

## Minimum size
- Lockup: 120px wide on screen, 1.25 in wide in print.
- Mark alone: 16px (favicon), 0.25 in in print.
- Below these sizes, use the mark alone.

## Color
- Ink (#1B2330) on primer or white.
- White on ink, or white on a photo only when placed on the reversed ink tile.
- Never in chalk blue, a family color or a trade color.

## Misuse
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline or add effects or shadows.
- Do not rebuild the wordmark in another font.
- Do not change the miter cut or fill in the gap.
- Do not place directly on busy photos.
- Do not put the trade name beside the wordmark; it always sits below.
- Do not animate the mark except the approved reveal: the two pieces slide together along the miter once, 400ms, then rest.

## Favicon and app icon
Mark in white on the ink tile with `radius-md` corners. Export 16, 32, 180 and 512 px.

## Status
The mark and wordmark are production-ready vectors for digital use. Before printing vehicle wraps or signage, have a designer do a final optical kerning pass and file the trademark.
