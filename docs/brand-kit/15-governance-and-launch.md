# Governance and launch

## Ownership
- **Final approval:** Kaleb approves every new application of the brand before it goes public: new pages, ads, vehicles, signs, merchandise.
- **This system is the source of truth.** If something is not covered, ask before inventing.

## File standards
- Logos: always use the files in the Logos asset group. Never screenshots.
- File names: `solvern_[item]_[trade]_[version].ext`, for example `solvern_yard-sign_roofing_v2.pdf`.
- Photos: `YYYY-MM-DD_neighborhood_trade_##.jpg`.

## Review checklist for anything new
1. Correct logo file, clear space and minimum size.
2. Only approved colors; one trade color at most.
3. Archivo and Public Sans only, sentence case.
4. Voice rules followed; no banned words.
5. Phone and Concept Preview clearly visible.
6. Contrast checked.
7. Name, address and phone consistent.

## Launch checklist (all trades live the same day)
**Name and accounts**
- Secure the domain, misspelling domains and @solvernhome handles.
- Register the Solvern Home trade name under the LLC in Georgia.
- Search and file the trademark.

**Brand assets**
- Final optical pass on the logo; export print files.
- Pantone and vinyl matches for vehicle and sign colors, with physical proofs.

**Website**
- Hub, 23 trade pages, 6 project pages, Concept Preview page, about, contact, work gallery.
- At least 4 neighborhood pages at launch.
- 23 tool models plus the hub tool wall, each within budget, with poster images.
- Analytics and call tracking installed.

**Local search**
- Google Business Profile verified with services, photos and hours.
- Listings on Apple Maps, Yelp, Houzz, Nextdoor and Angi with identical details.

**Operations**
- Concept Preview intake, 48-hour production routine and tracking in place.
- Estimate, proposal and invoice templates.
- Phone greeting and voicemail recorded.
- Uniforms, business cards, yard signs and vehicle graphics ordered.

## Open items
