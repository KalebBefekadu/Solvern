# Photography and renderings

## Project photography
- **Subject:** finished work first, then the craft in progress (hands, tools, precise details), then the team.
- **Light:** natural daylight, true colors, no heavy filters. Interiors shot bright and level.
- **Composition:** straight verticals, level horizons, clean and staged spaces. Shoot wide, detail and close-up for every project.
- **Before and after:** same angle, same lens height, same time of day where possible. Shown side by side or with a slider.
- **People:** real Solvern team members in uniform, working carefully and cleanly. Homeowners only with written permission.
- **Retouching:** remove clutter and cords; never alter the work itself.

## Never
- Stock photos of smiling contractors, handshakes or hard-hat thumbs up.
- Dirty, unsafe or messy job sites.
- Photos of competitors' trucks, signs or branded tools.
- Heavy vignettes, color filters or HDR effects.

## Concept Preview images
- Always labeled "Concept Preview" in the corner with a small `ink` badge.
- Shown next to the customer's original photo, same crop.
- Caption: "Concept image for planning. Final materials and measurements are confirmed at your site visit."
- Never used on the website or in ads without the homeowner's written permission, and never presented as a finished project.

## Photo checklist for every job
1. Before: 3 wide angles, 3 details.
2. During: 2 craft details per day of major work.
3. After: the same 3 wide angles as before, 5 details, 1 twilight or golden-hour exterior when relevant.
4. Upload to the shared project folder named `YYYY-MM-DD_neighborhood_trade`.
