# SEO and local search

## Principles
- The name Solvern carries brand searches. Trade, project and neighborhood pages carry keyword searches.
- One domain for everything, so every page and review strengthens the whole site.

## Page titles and descriptions
| Page | Title format | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hub | Solvern Home: [promise] in metro Atlanta | Solvern Home: Home renovation and every trade in metro Atlanta |
| Trade | [Service] in Atlanta, GA \| Solvern [Trade] | Hardwood and LVP flooring installation in Atlanta, GA \| Solvern Flooring |
| Project | [Project] in Atlanta \| Solvern Home | Kitchen remodeling in Atlanta \| Solvern Home |
| Neighborhood | [Trade or remodeling] in [Area], GA \| Solvern | Home remodeling in Buckhead, GA \| Solvern |
Meta descriptions: 140 to 155 characters, name the service, the area and the Concept Preview.

## URLs
Short, lowercase, hyphenated: /flooring, /projects/kitchen-remodeling, /areas/buckhead. Never change a live URL without a redirect.

## Google Business Profile
- One profile: Solvern Home.
- Primary category: General contractor. Add the most relevant additional categories allowed.
- List every trade as a service with a short description from the Trade directory.
- Photos weekly: finished work, crews in uniform, vehicles.
- Ask every customer for a review at the final walkthrough. Reply to every review within 2 days in the brand voice.
- Service area: the metro Atlanta counties served.

## Content
- Every trade page: at least 600 words of useful, specific content, plus FAQs.
- Neighborhood pages: real projects from that area, not copied text with the area name swapped.
- Use structured data: LocalBusiness on every page, Service on trade pages, FAQPage on FAQs, Review where allowed.

## Consistency
Name, address and phone identical everywhere: website, Google, Apple Maps, Yelp, Houzz, Nextdoor, Angi, social profiles.
