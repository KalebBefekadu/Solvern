# Touchpoints

## Vehicles
- Body: `ink` or white vehicle. On white: ink lockup large on the sides, a band in one family color along the lower edge only if the vehicle serves one family, otherwise no color band.
- Sides: lockup, "See it first. Built right.", phone, solvern.com.
- Rear: mark, phone, website. Large enough to read at 50 feet.
- Hood or roof: mark alone for overhead visibility.
- No photos, no lists of trades, no clip art.

## Yard signs
- 18 x 24 in, white background, ink lockup, the trade name if one trade is on site, "Another project built right," phone and website.
- One `chalk-blue` dimension line across the sign measuring the lockup.

## Uniforms
- Shirts: ink with the white mark on the chest, "Solvern" on the back. Hats: ink with the white mark.
- Hi-vis or safety gear: mark only, following safety color requirements.
- Always clean; replace faded items.

## Business cards
- 3.5 x 2 in. Front: lockup on primer. Back: name, title, phone, email, website, and a dimension line measuring the card.

## Estimates, proposals and invoices
- White page, lockup top left, document title in `heading-2`.
- Line items in a table with tabular figures, `chalk-line` rules, totals in bold.
- Include the Concept Preview image on the first page of every proposal where one was made.
- Footer: legal name, address, phone, website.

## Email signature
Name (bold), title, Solvern Home, phone, website. Mark at 32px. No quotes, banners or colored backgrounds.

## Social media
- Avatar: reversed mark tile.
- Posts: before and after, craft details, Concept Preview next to the finished result (with permission), team at work.
- Every graphic uses primer or ink grounds, Archivo headlines and one trade color at most.
- Captions follow the voice rules. No hashtag walls; 3 to 5 relevant tags.

## Hard hats and equipment
White or ink hard hats with the mark on the front. Equipment and trailers: mark and phone.

## Job site
Clean site standard: materials stacked, debris removed daily, yard sign placed at the curb. The site is a brand touchpoint.

## Phone
Greeting: "Thank you for calling Solvern, this is [name]. How can I help with your home?" Voicemail repeats the Concept Preview offer and the website.
