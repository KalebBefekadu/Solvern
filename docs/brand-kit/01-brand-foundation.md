# Brand foundation

## Purpose
Make improving a home feel clear, confident and certain.

## Mission
Deliver precise, high-quality work across every trade a home needs, with one accountable team and a process homeowners can see and trust from the first photo to the final walkthrough.

## Vision
Be the name metro Atlanta homeowners call first for anything their home needs, because Solvern is the company that shows you the result before you commit and then builds exactly that.

## Positioning statement
For homeowners in metro Atlanta who want work done right, Solvern Home is the construction company that handles any trade or an entire project with one team. Unlike contractors who ask you to imagine the result, Solvern shows you your project before it is built and runs every job with the precision of a well-planned build.

## Brand promise
See it first. Built right.

## Differentiators
- **The Concept Preview.** Send a photo and a description; get back a concept image of the finished project and an estimate.
- **One team for everything.** 23 trades under one name, one point of contact, one standard of quality.
- **Precision in the process.** Clear timelines, line-by-line estimates and updates at every stage.
- **Premium without the runaround.** High-end work and presentation at a fair, clearly explained price.

## Values
- **Precise.** We measure twice, quote clearly and finish what we promise.
- **Clear.** Customers always know what is happening, what it costs and what comes next.
- **Accountable.** One team owns the result. No finger pointing between trades.
- **Crafted.** The finish matters, including the parts nobody sees.
- **Respectful.** Of the home, the schedule and the budget.

## Personality: Modern Craft

| --- | --- |

| Confident | Boastful or loud |
| Modern | Flashy or techy |
| Expert | Jargon-heavy |

## Archetype
Primary: **the Creator** (master craftsman who makes something lasting). Secondary: **the Sage** (the expert who explains clearly). Together: the builder who knows exactly what they are doing and shows you why.

## Primary customer
- Homeowners in higher-value metro Atlanta neighborhoods, typically busy professionals and families who value their time.
- Anyone else willing to pay well for work done right.

What they fear: no-shows, surprise costs, poor communication, unfinished work, and not knowing what the result will look like.
What they want: certainty, a beautiful result, one reliable team, and to be kept informed without chasing anyone.

## Two customer jobs
1. **A single-trade job.** "My fence is falling down and I want a better one." They land on a trade page, want proof of expertise and a fast estimate.
2. **A full project.** "We want to redo the kitchen and open up the main floor." They land on the hub, want confidence that one team can handle everything.
The brand must serve both, and turn every single-trade customer into a future full-project customer.
