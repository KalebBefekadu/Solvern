# Solvern Home

Solvern Home is a residential construction company serving metro Atlanta. One accountable team handles 23 trades, from a single repair to a complete renovation. This book is the source of truth for how the brand looks, sounds and behaves everywhere: the website, the 3D trade tools, trucks, signs, uniforms, estimates and every message we send.

Tagline: **See it first. Built right.**

## The brand in one page

**Who we serve.** Homeowners in higher-value neighborhoods, and anyone who values great work at a fair price.

**Price position.** Accessible premium. It looks and feels high end without pricing out people who want it done right.

**Personality.** Modern Craft: precise, clean, confident, expert. We read like a master builder who is also extremely organized.

**What makes us different.** Customers see their project before we build it (the Concept Preview), and one team can take any trade or the whole project.

**The quiet edge.** Solvern runs like a next-era, technology-enabled company, but the brand never says so. Customers experience speed, clarity and precision. Craft is always the headline; technology is only the proof. See Voice and messaging for the banned word list.

**Architecture.** A branded house. One name, Solvern, in front of every trade: Solvern Roofing, Solvern Tile, Solvern Landscape. The hub, Solvern Home, sells complete projects and routes visitors to each trade.

## Content fundamentals

- Sentence case everywhere, including headings and buttons. The logo is the only all-caps text.
- Short, plain sentences. Specific numbers over adjectives: "Concept and estimate within 48 hours," not "fast turnaround."
- Speak to the homeowner as "you." Refer to the company as "we" or "Solvern," never "Solvern Home Services LLC" in marketing copy.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no superlatives we cannot prove ("best," "#1").
- Buttons say exactly what happens: "Get my Concept Preview," "Call Solvern," "See the project."

## Visual foundations

- **Ground:** `primer` (#EEF0EE) for pages, `white` for cards, forms and documents.
- **Ink:** `ink` (#1B2330) for text, the logo and the hub's dominant color.
- **One accent:** `chalk-blue` (#2F63D6), the color of a builder's chalk line. Used for dimension lines, links and focus, never as a big fill.
- **Color families:** six families group the 23 trades so customers learn where each trade lives in the house. Every trade has its own shade (`trade-*` tokens).
- **Type:** Archivo (display, wide and sturdy like job-site signage) and Public Sans (body, plain and highly readable).
- **Shapes:** tight radii (`radius-sm` 2px to `radius-lg` 8px). Structure comes from hairlines and measured spacing, not shadows or gradients.
- **Signature device:** dimension lines. Thin chalk-blue lines with end ticks that "measure" photos, tools and sections, as on a blueprint.
- **Signature object:** a 3D trade tool for every trade, rendered in one consistent style.

## Iconography

Line icons at 1.5px stroke on a 24px grid, square caps and joins, drawn in `ink` or a family deep token. Icons label; they never decorate. Trade identity comes from the 3D tools and trade colors, not from icons. No filled, cartoon or multicolor icons.

## Rules that are never broken

1. Never use the words AI, artificial intelligence, powered by, smart, algorithm, cutting-edge, next-gen, revolutionary or disrupt in anything a customer sees.
2. Never place the logo on a busy photo without the reversed tile.
3. Never set body text in a trade color. Use `ink`, `graphite` or a family deep token.
4. Never let the 3D tool delay the phone number or the Concept Preview button.
5. Kaleb approves every new brand application before it goes public.
