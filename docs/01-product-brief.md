# 01. Product brief

## The business
Solvern Home is a residential construction company serving all of metro Atlanta. One accountable team covers 23 trades, from a single repair to a complete renovation. It operates as one LLC with registered trade names.

## Brand architecture
Branded house. One name, **Solvern**, in front of every trade: Solvern Carpentry, Solvern HVAC, Solvern Masonry. The main site, **Solvern Home**, sells complete general contracting projects and routes visitors to each trade.

## The 23 trades
Demolition, Site Work / Excavation, Concrete / Foundation / Driveway, Masonry, Carpentry, Roofing & Gutters, Siding / Exterior Cladding, Windows & Doors, Garage Doors, Insulation, Drywall, Painting, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC / Mechanical, Low Voltage / Smart Home, Flooring, Tile, Countertops, Landscaping / Irrigation, Tree Service, Fencing & Gates, Specialty Trades. All go live the same day. Details: `content/trades.json`.

## Customer
Homeowners in higher-value metro Atlanta neighborhoods, plus anyone willing to pay well for work done right. They fear no-shows, surprise costs, poor communication, unfinished work and not knowing what the result will look like.

## Positioning
- **Price:** accessible premium. High-end look and feel without pricing out people who want it done right.
- **Personality:** Modern Craft. Precise, clean, confident, expert.
- **Quiet edge:** a next-era, technology-enabled company that never says so. Customers experience speed, clarity and precision. Craft is always the headline.
- **Tagline:** See it first. Built right.
- **Differentiator:** the Concept Preview (send a photo, get a concept image and estimate within 48 hours) plus one team for any trade or the whole project.

## Conversion goals
1. Phone calls.
2. Concept Preview requests (visual trades) or visit requests (diagnosis trades: plumbing, electrical, HVAC, smart home).
3. Callback requests through the customer service page.
